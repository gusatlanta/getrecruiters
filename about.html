Our Story

Recruiting Is What We Do — Now It Works for You

GetRecruiters Group is a division of NorthPoint Search Group. We've been recruiting for more than 20 years, and we know how hard it is to find great people while you're busy running the business.

Demand for recruiters is up everywhere — especially in data centers and mission-critical construction, the skilled trades, healthcare, public accounting, and financial services. So we put experienced recruiters on your openings: a dedicated virtual recruiter who works as part of your team, or recruiters you hire onto your own staff.

Either way, you get recruiters who already know your industry — and a team behind them that has done this thousands of times.
