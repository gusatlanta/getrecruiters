Temp Recruiters

Need extra recruiting hands for a hiring surge, a new site, or a data center build? Our temp recruiters step in quickly — screened and ready to work your reqs from day one.

  • Rapid deployment — often within 48–72 hours
  • Assignments from a few weeks to 12+ months
  • We handle payroll, taxes, and workers' comp
  • Recruiters who already know your industry
  • Scale a whole recruiting team up or down
Request a Temp Recruiter

Best For

  • Hiring surges and seasonal peaks
  • New plants, sites, and data center builds
  • Leave and vacancy coverage
  • Backlogs of open requisitions
  • Pilots before adding headcount
Fee Structure
Bill rate per hour — no placement fee
Temp-to-Perm

Temp-to-Perm Recruiters

Start a recruiter as a temp and move them to your payroll when you're sure of the fit. It lowers the risk for both sides — and works well while headcount approval is in progress.

  • Typical temp period of 90–180 days
  • They work on your team from day one
  • Conversion fee agreed upfront
  • Ideal when headcount approval is in progress
  • They learn your hiring managers before converting
Explore Temp-to-Perm

Best For

  • First recruiter for a growing company
  • New positions with evolving scope
  • Teams under a hiring freeze
  • Roles where team fit is critical
  • Building a recruiting function from scratch
Fee Structure
Bill rate as a temp; reduced conversion fee when you hire them
Direct Hire

Direct Hire Recruiters

When you need the right recruiter or talent leader on your team for the long haul, we deliver a curated shortlist of qualified, motivated candidates — fast.

  • Deep intake call — roles, hiring managers, ATS, and team
  • Active and passive candidate sourcing
  • Screening questionnaire answers with every résumé
  • Reference and background coordination
  • Offer negotiation and onboarding support
  • Replacement guarantee included

Best For

  • Talent acquisition managers and directors
  • Heads of talent and recruiting operations
  • Sourcers and recruiting coordinators
  • HR generalists and HR business partners
