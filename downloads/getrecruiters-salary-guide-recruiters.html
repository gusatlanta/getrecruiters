What to do

Ways to hire better this year

  1. Price to the ask. Put a pay range in the post. For Recruiter / TA, the middle half of asks is $70K to $95K (based on 229 people).
  2. Say how you pay. 95% want a full-time salaried job (based on 165 people), and 49% would take a 1099 commission-only job (based on 143 people). Put the base and the commission plan in the post.
  3. Ask what they billed. The typical recruiter who told us billed $130K in their most recent year (based on 34 people). Ask for the number, not just the years.
  4. Hire for full-cycle. 54% have 5 or more years of full-cycle recruiting (based on 441 people). Ask how many searches they've run from first call to start date.
  5. Offer hybrid. 91% will work hybrid (based on 217 people).

Method and sources

Everything on these pages comes from job applications and screening questionnaires that people filled out for NorthPoint Search Group between January 1, 2024 and October 22, 2026: 737 people, one form per person (their newest). Pay is the desired base salary each person gave us; the middle half is the 25th to 75th percentile, rounded to $500. Every number says how many people it rests on. Groups with fewer than 5 people are left out, and no one's individual answer is shown.

This guide counts everyone who filled out one of our recruiter applications, plus anyone else whose current title is recruiter, sourcer or talent acquisition.

  • BLS Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey, 2025 annual averages (released March 13, 2026), Tables 20 and 22.
  • BLS Employee Tenure, median years with current employer by occupation, January 2026, Table 6.
