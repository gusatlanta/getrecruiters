Join Our Partner Program

You know the companies that are hiring. Put those relationships to work — you bring the need, we do all the recruiting, and you get paid.

For HR Leaders, Consultants & Anyone Whose Network Hires

You get the order. We fill it.

You get paid for every hour someone works.

You get the order

Hear "we need recruiting help" from someone in your network — and make the introduction.

We fill it

We put a virtual recruiter on their openings, or place recruiters on their team — temp, temp-to-perm, or direct hire.

You get paid

A referral fee on direct hire placements, and a recurring share of every hour your client's temps work — for the life of the assignment.

What You Earn

Simple, generous, recurring.

10%

Direct hire placements

Your client hires direct through us — you earn 10% of the placement fee we collect. Paid when we collect from the client.

25%

Temp net spread

Your client runs temps or temp-to-perm workers — you earn 25% of the net spread on every hour they bill.

Recurring

While they work

Temp commissions aren't one-time. They keep paying as long as your client's temps keep billing. Build a book.

15th

Paid every month

Commissions are calculated on money actually received and paid on the 15th of the following month. Every dollar tracked for you.

Who This Is For

Anyone whose network hires.

HR leaders and consultants. CFOs and CPA firm partners. Construction, data center, and healthcare executives. Retired executives — anyone whose network is always hiring. You never become a recruiter; you bring the need and stay the relationship owner, and our team fills it.

How It Works

Four simple steps.

  1. Apply below — we review every partner personally and set you up.
  2. Hear "we need a recruiter" (or a whole recruiting team for a new site) — send it in.
  3. We fill it — a virtual recruiter, or temp, temp-to-perm, or direct hire.
  4. You're paid on the 15th of the following month — 10% of the fee on direct hire, 25% of net spread on temps, recurring while the worker bills.

Net spread = what the client pays, minus worker wages and employer burden (taxes, workers comp, benefits, compliance). Commissions are calculated on payments actually received; refunded or unpaid invoices are reversed. Self-referrals and companies already under agreement with us don't qualify. Earned commissions are never reduced.
